# AI Collaboration Log


# Project:

Vinhomes AI Resident Feedback Intelligence System

# How AI Supported


AI assistant was used for:


## 1. Problem Discovery

AI helped identify operational bottlenecks inside Vinhomes ecosystem.


## 2. Solution Design

AI suggested:

- LLM classification
- Workflow automation
- Human-in-the-loop design


## 3. Prompt Engineering


Initial prompt:

"Create a chatbot for Vinhomes"


Problem:

The scope was too broad.


Improved prompt:


"Act as AI Product Engineer. Design an AI system that classifies resident feedback and recommends operation actions."


Result:

More focused solution.



---

# AI Limitation


Potential hallucination:


AI may invent:

- Incorrect policies
- Incorrect operation procedures


Solution:


Added constraints:

- AI only recommends
- Human approval required
- Use verified knowledge base
