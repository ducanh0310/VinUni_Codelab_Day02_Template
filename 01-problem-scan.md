# Problem Card 1 ⭐ Selected

## Vinhomes AI Resident Feedback Intelligence System


### Actor

Primary:

- Vinhomes Operation Team
- Customer Service Staff
- Building Management


Secondary:

- Residents


---

### Current Workflow


Resident submits complaint

↓

Customer service receives message

↓

Staff manually classify issue

↓

Assign department

↓

Follow up resolution


---

### Bottleneck


1. High manual workload

2. Slow issue routing

3. Inconsistent classification

4. Difficult to identify repeated problems


---

### AI Solution


AI Resident Feedback Intelligence Engine:


- NLP understanding
- Issue classification
- Priority detection
- Department recommendation
- Trend analysis


---

### Success Metrics


- Reduce classification time by 80%
- Routing accuracy >90%
- Faster response for urgent complaints
